# Cinematic Storyworld — Prompt Templates

## 1. Character reference sheet
Create a clean character turnaround/reference sheet for an original animated character. The character has a simple pale round face, minimal dark eyes and mouth, expressive brows, and a readable silhouette. Integrate the graphic face language with a dimensional stylized 3D body and clothing. Show front, side, three-quarter and back views, plus neutral, happy, worried, angry, shocked and sad expressions. Preserve identical head shape, eye spacing, costume, body proportions and colors in every view. Neutral studio background, even lighting, full body visible, no text, no watermark, no extra limbs.

## 2. Cinematic 3D environment keyframe
Vertical 9:16 cinematic stylized 3D animated-film frame of [LOCATION]. Include [ARCHITECTURAL DETAILS], [PROPS], and clear foreground, midground and background layers. Lighting: [TIME OF DAY / MOOD]. Color palette: [PALETTE]. Camera: [SHOT SIZE], [ANGLE], [LENS FEEL], with a clear focal subject at [POSITION]. Rich material detail, coherent architecture, soft atmospheric depth, polished 3D rendering, readable composition for a phone screen. No captions, no logos, no watermark, no random text.

## 3. Main character in environment
Use the supplied character reference exactly. Place [CHARACTER] in [LOCATION], performing [ONE CLEAR ACTION]. Preserve face design, eye spacing, costume, colors, body proportions and scale. The environment is a polished stylized 3D animated world. Include contact shadow and lighting that matches the environment. Camera: [SHOT TYPE / ANGLE]. Emotion: [EXPRESSION]. Keep hands/props readable and avoid occlusion. Vertical 9:16, no captions, no watermark.

## 4. Large creature / dramatic subject
A cinematic close-up or medium shot of [CREATURE / OBJECT] in [ENVIRONMENT]. Emphasize scale, silhouette, surface materials, ornamental details and a clear focal point. Lighting: [LIGHTING]. Camera: [LOW ANGLE / EXTREME CLOSE-UP / TRACKING]. Maintain the supplied design reference. The subject must remain anatomically and structurally coherent. No text, no logo, no watermark.

## 5. 10-second image-to-video clip
Animate the supplied first frame into a 10-second continuous cinematic shot. Preserve exact character identity, costume, environment layout, lighting and object design. Action: [START ACTION] develops naturally into [END ACTION]. Camera: [ONE SPECIFIC MOVE, e.g. slow dolly-in with slight left-to-right track]. Add subtle secondary motion only where appropriate: [CLOTH / FIRE / DUST / LEAVES / CROWD]. Keep motion physically coherent and avoid morphing, face drift, extra limbs, object duplication, sudden scene changes, text, subtitles or watermark. End with a composition that can connect to the next clip.

## 6. Connected next clip
Continue seamlessly from the previous clip's final frame. Preserve character appearance, screen direction, environment, lighting, props and camera axis. New action: [NEXT ACTION]. Camera transition: [CUT / MATCH CUT / CONTINUOUS TRACK / PUSH-IN]. Do not reset character positions unless the script explicitly requires it. Keep the same visual style and avoid visual drift.

## 7. Shot storyboard prompt
Create a storyboard for a [DURATION] vertical animated sequence. For each shot specify: timecode, narration phrase, shot size, camera angle, camera movement, environment, character action, facial expression, key prop, lighting, transition and sound cue. Use a new visual beat every 1–3 seconds. Alternate wide, medium, close-up, extreme close-up, overhead and insert shots. Every shot must visualize a specific narration phrase. Keep the main character and environment designs consistent. Do not include unrequested dialogue or plot details.

## 8. Thumbnail prompt
Create a high-contrast vertical/YouTube thumbnail composition based on [VIDEO IDEA]. One dominant expressive character or creature, one instantly readable conflict or mystery, dramatic cinematic lighting, clean uncluttered background, strong silhouette and clear focal hierarchy. Leave clean negative space for title text to be added later. No generated text, logos or watermark.
