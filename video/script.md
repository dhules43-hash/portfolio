## Fact map
- 1453 siege of Constantinople; Mehmed II born 1432, so 21 in spring 1453.
- Chain (boom) across the Golden Horn on floating logs/pontoons; Ottoman ships could not break it.
- Road of greased logs behind Galata (Pera); ships hauled on wheeled cradles by men and oxen.
- About 70 ships (sources say 70-80) relaunched into the Golden Horn on 22 April 1453.

## Left out on purpose
- Exact route length (sources give 1.5-3 km) and "in one night" (sources disagree).
- The admiral's name; the frying-pan line is a joke, not a quote.

## Wording to watch
- "about seventy" covers the 70-80 range.
